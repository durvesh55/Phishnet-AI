Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what this tool can and can't do.

Do you visit the link I paste?

No. Links are analysed as text only. We never fetch, resolve or open them, so checking a link cannot expose you to it. The trade-off is that we cannot see the live page or check external blocklists.

Does "no indicators found" mean it is safe?

No. It means our rules and model found nothing suspicious. New or well-crafted phishing can pass. When in doubt, go to the organisation's site by typing the address yourself.

How accurate is the machine-learning score?

The score is a model-derived risk indicator (phishing probability × 100), not a calibrated guarantee. It is only as good as its training data, and the default model is a demonstration model.

Are my emails stored?

Quick checks on this page are not stored. Signed-in scans are stored in your account only, and you can disable text storage, set automatic deletion, export or delete everything.

Why is a bare link not given an ML score?

The model learns from email wording. A link alone has too little text, so only the rule-based link analysis is shown.

What should I do with a real phishing email?

Do not click anything or reply. Report it to your IT team or mail provider, and change your password if you already entered it anywhere.
