What we process

  • Account: name, email, salted password hash, sign-in security events.
  • Scans: the text you submit (unless you disable storage), its SHA-256 hash and length, the ML result, the rule findings and model version.
  • Feedback & API keys: your correctness feedback; API key hashes (never raw keys), usage counters.

What we do not do

  • Submitted emails are analysed locally and are not sent to external AI services.
  • Links inside emails are never opened or fetched.
  • Your emails and feedback are not used to train models without your explicit consent.

Your controls

  • Delete individual scans or your whole account at any time.
  • Export all your data as JSON.
  • Choose a retention period or disable storage of email text in Account settings.
